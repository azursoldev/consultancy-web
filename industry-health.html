Healthcare & Life Sciences

Protecting ultra-sensitive patient diagnostics, electronic medical records (EHR), and genomic telemetry across hospitals, HMOs, and clinical research environments.

Clinical confidentiality in a connected digital health network

Healthcare data constitutes the most sensitive category of personal data under NDPA Section 30. Patient diagnostics, prescription logs, genetic markers, and biometric identifiers cannot be processed without explicit consent or statutory medical emergency exemptions.

Amstel Consulting assists teaching hospitals, HMO underwriters, diagnostic chains, and telemedicine innovators in structuring impenetrable EHR security postures, ensuring compliance with both the National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA) and NDPC.

EHR Encryption, Telemedicine & Clinical Trial Anonymization

As clinical research expands across West Africa, cross-border transmission of clinical trial participant dossiers requires certified pseudonymization and differential privacy safeguards. Telemedicine consultations require HIPAA/NDPA compliant encrypted channels.

Our healthcare advisory practice drafts specialized Patient Consent Charters, establishes role-based EHR access matrices, and conducts statutory DPIAs on AI-assisted diagnostic imaging tools.

Explore Healthcare Advisory Services

HMO Claims Data Lineage & Third-Party Laboratory DPAs

Health Maintenance Organizations (HMOs) sit at the center of complex multi-party data transfers between corporate employers, primary clinics, diagnostic imaging labs, and pharmaceutical dispensaries.

We map patient data lineage across all ecosystem participants, closing contractual liability gaps and ensuring medical claims records comply with statutory retention and destruction schedules.

Electronic Medical Record (EMR) Access Audits

Rigorous logging, audit trails, and multi-factor authentication controls to stop unauthorized staff snooping into sensitive celebrity and VIP health records.

Statutory Health Sector CAR Audit Assurance

Preparing private healthcare operators and pharmaceutical manufacturers for the annual March 15 statutory NDPC audit return certification.

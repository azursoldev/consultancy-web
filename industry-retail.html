Retail & E-Commerce

Managing omnichannel customer data, loyalty telemetry, zero-cookie compliance, and third-party delivery privacy across FMCG, retail chains, and digital marketplaces.

Customer loyalty meets zero-cookie consent architecture

Modern retail thrives on customer analytics: purchasing history, geofenced offers, loyalty card scanning, and website cookies. However, indiscriminate tracking without granular consent violates both NDPA 2023 and FCCPC Consumer Protection directives.

Amstel Consulting helps major FMCG retailers, supermarket chains, and e-commerce platforms design frictionless, transparent consent experiences that increase brand trust while securing full regulatory immunity.

Third-Party Delivery Dispatchers & SMS/Email Marketing Compliance

Sharing customer home addresses, telephone numbers, and order notes with third-party logistics dispatchers creates substantial breach liability unless governed by strict Data Processing Agreements.

We audit e-commerce fulfillment pipelines, establish automated number-masking protocols for courier communication, and implement legally compliant opt-out mechanisms for promotional SMS blasts.

Explore Retail Privacy Solutions

Omnichannel POS security & Payment Card Industry safeguards

Physical Point-of-Sale (POS) terminals in retail outlets collect massive transactional records daily. Ensuring end-to-end tokenization from in-store swipe to cloud accounting is essential.

Our team performs full GAID compliance audits across retail chains, reviewing in-store CCTV retention policies and customer surveillance disclosures.

Zero-Cookie & Banner Consent Architecture

Eliminating non-consensual tracking pixels and Google Analytics scripts prior to explicit consumer opt-in on e-commerce web storefronts.

Retail Chain Annual CAR Audit Assurance

Preparing omnichannel supermarket and department store chains for annual March 15 NDPC compliance certification and audit return filings.

Esther Samson

Data Privacy Training Consultant

Esther Samson is a Data Privacy Training Consultant who draws on more than five years of experience within Nigeria’s banking and financial-services sector to support the delivery of practical and accessible privacy training.

Her banking experience includes roles with United Bank for Africa and First City Monument Bank. In these environments, she worked with customer records, financial information, account documentation and confidential enquiries, giving her direct insight into the importance of responsible information handling.

Esther’s experience has also exposed her to customer identification and onboarding processes, KYC requirements, accurate record keeping, complaint resolution and the careful use of information in customer-service operations. She understands how everyday employee actions can affect the confidentiality, accuracy and security of personal data.

At Amstel Consulting, Esther applies this operational banking experience to data-privacy awareness and training delivery. She helps translate privacy principles into clear, relatable examples that employees can connect with their daily responsibilities.

Her contribution is particularly valuable when training customer-facing employees and teams responsible for collecting, reviewing or maintaining personal information. She supports learners in understanding why privacy requirements matter and how mistakes in routine activities can create risks for individuals and organisations.

Esther’s practical approach helps move privacy training beyond legal terminology. Through realistic workplace examples, she supports employees in recognising personal data, handling customer information responsibly, escalating concerns and applying appropriate safeguards during their everyday work.

She also contributes to the organisation and delivery of training sessions, including participant coordination, learning materials, practical exercises and learner engagement. Her client-service background helps her communicate professionally with participants from different roles and levels of experience.

Qualifications & Education

Esther holds a Bachelor of Science in Banking and Financial Support Services from Nasarawa State University, Keffi, and a National Diploma in Banking and Finance from Federal Polytechnic, Nasarawa.

File Your Annual Compliance Audit Return (CAR) Safely & On-Time

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), any organization that processes the personal data of over 2,000 data subjects within a 12-month period is statutorily mandated to file an annual Compliance Audit Return (CAR) with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). This critical statutory filing serves as your official corporate proof of data compliance to regulatory authorities. However, under Nigerian law, organizations cannot file this return independently; the process must be evaluated, verified, and officially submitted through a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organization (DPCO) like Amstel Consulting.

Missing the annual March 15th filing deadline poses severe operational and financial risks to your organization. Failing to file your CAR on time automatically flags your business for direct, intrusive regulatory enforcement audits and exposes your company to heavy statutory fines calculated against your annual corporate turnover. Furthermore, a lack of a validated filing history can lead to regulatory blacklisting, meaning your organization could be disqualified from bidding on government contracts or passing mandatory vendor risk assessments.

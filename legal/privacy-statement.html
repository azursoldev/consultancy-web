ARTICLE 27 NDPA 2023 • STATUTORY NOTICE

Protecting Your Personal Data with Institutional Integrity.

Contact DPO Desk

Amstel Consulting Ltd ("we", "us", or "our") operates as a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO) under the statutory regulatory oversight of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). This Corporate Privacy Statement details our binding fiduciary commitments regarding the collection, processing, protection, and retention of personal data collected through our digital interfaces and professional advisory engagements.

We process personal data strictly in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable to international clients, and governing professional non-disclosure regulations.

Lawful Basis of Processing

Under Section 25 of the NDPA 2023, all personal data processed by Amstel Consulting is anchored upon explicit, lawful grounds:

  • Consent: Freely given, specific, and unambiguous consent obtained when you request a formal compliance audit proposal or newsletter updates.
  • Contractual Necessity: Processing necessary for the performance of a statutory DPCO audit, CAR filing verification, or Outsourced DPO retainer agreement.
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with statutory reporting, anti-money laundering (AML), and regulatory audit mandates issued by the NDPC or relevant fiscal authorities.
  • Legitimate Interests: Protecting the security, availability, and resilience of our corporate infrastructure against cyber threats and unauthorized access.

Data Subject Statutory Rights

Under Part VI of the NDPA 2023, you retain sovereign rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, rectify inaccuracies, request erasure ("right to be forgotten"), object to processing, and request portability of your records. You also maintain the statutory right to lodge an administrative complaint directly with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

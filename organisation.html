Our Organisation: About Amstel Consulting

1. Leading with Integrity and Resilience

Amstel Consulting Ltd is a premier, licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO) and cybersecurity advisory firm dedicated to securing the digital assets of corporate enterprises. Founded on the principles of regulatory integrity and operational resilience, we bridge the critical gap between complex statutory data laws and daily business activities. We firmly believe that corporate data compliance should never be a bottleneck to business growth; rather, it should serve as an ironclad foundation that builds trust, protects consumer rights, and drives sustainable long-term enterprise value.

2. Plain-Language Compliance and Corporate Strategy

Our core mission is to strip away the complex technical jargon and legal intimidation surrounding data governance, translating rigid frameworks into straightforward, actionable corporate strategies. By combining elite legal advisory expertise with hands-on technical defense engineering, we insulate businesses from catastrophic data breaches, heavy regulatory enforcement penalties, and long-term brand erosion. We operate transparently, maintaining absolute confidentiality and professionalism to ensure our corporate clients navigate the evolving digital compliance ecosystem with total peace of mind.

AMSTEL CONSULTING IS AN NDPC LICENSED DPCO

3. Our Credentials as a Licensed DPCO

What truly sets Amstel Consulting apart is our elite, dual-capability professional status. We operate under strict regulatory licensing as an authorized DPCO, giving us the official corporate authority to process, verify, and legally submit mandatory annual Compliance Audit Returns (CAR) directly to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). This official regulatory backing ensures that when you partner with us, your filings, corporate governance metrics, and internal privacy practices carry absolute institutional weight and validity.

4. A Multidisciplinary Team of Senior Advisory Experts

Our capability is driven entirely by our multidisciplinary Advisory Board and executive consultants. We bring together seasoned data privacy lawyers, veteran cybersecurity architects, and corporate governance specialists who have actively secured complex digital environments across the banking, fintech, healthcare, and logistics sectors. Rather than separating our leadership profiles onto disconnected web routes, our combined team operates under a singular framework, ensuring your organization receives comprehensive boardroom-level guidance and rapid incident support.

Martha Jowah, Esq

Head of Legal & Privacy Compliance

Martha is an accomplished legal practitioner with expertise in corporate and regulatory compliance, data protection and privacy, contract management and legal risk. As Head of Legal & Privacy Compliance, she brings a strong legal perspective to Amstel Consulting’s multidisciplinary approach to privacy, governance and regulatory compliance.

She advises on data protection and privacy matters, helping organisations understand their regulatory obligations, manage privacy-related risks and embed effective data protection practices across their operations.

Her experience extends to drafting and reviewing complex commercial and technology agreements, developing contractual frameworks for government systems, conducting legal and regulatory research, and advising on compliance matters across diverse industries.

With a practical and commercially focused approach, Martha combines legal expertise with strong analytical, negotiation and communication skills to develop solutions that protect organisational interests while supporting sound governance and effective business operations.

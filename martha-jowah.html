Martha Jowah, Esq

Head of Legal & Privacy Compliance

Martha is an accomplished legal practitioner with expertise in corporate and regulatory compliance, data protection and privacy, contract management, legal research and risk management. As Head of Legal, she provides strategic legal guidance to organisations, helping them navigate complex regulatory requirements while strengthening governance, compliance and operational resilience.

She advises on data protection and privacy matters, supporting organisations in understanding their compliance obligations, managing privacy-related legal risks and embedding appropriate data protection practices within their operations.

Martha's experience also includes advising on legal and regulatory compliance, drafting and reviewing complex commercial and technology agreements, developing standard contractual frameworks for government systems, and analysing intricate legal and regulatory matters across diverse industries.

With a practical and commercially focused approach, Martha combines legal expertise with strong negotiation, analytical and communication skills to deliver solutions that protect organisational interests while supporting effective business operations.

Femi Leslie

IT Network and Infrastructure Consultant

Femi Leslie is an information-technology professional with practical experience in network operations, infrastructure support and the maintenance of business-critical technology environments.

Through his work as an IT Network Operator with Alva Dredging and Construction Limited, Femi has developed a strong understanding of the technology requirements of operationally demanding organisations. His experience includes supporting network availability, maintaining connectivity, responding to technical issues and helping users access the systems and resources required for their work.

Femi brings an operational perspective to Amstel Consulting’s technology and data-protection work. He understands that information-security and privacy controls must function within real organisational environments, where system reliability, appropriate user access and continuity of operations are essential.

His contribution helps connect data-protection requirements with practical infrastructure considerations. This includes examining how information is accessed, transmitted, stored and protected across organisational networks, devices and business systems.

Femi supports assignments involving network security, access management, infrastructure risk and the implementation of technical controls. His experience helps Amstel Consulting assess whether recommended security measures can be applied effectively within an organisation’s existing technology environment.

For clients, Femi’s involvement supports the development of practical recommendations that recognise operational realities. He helps identify technical weaknesses, assess infrastructure-related risks and strengthen the controls used to protect personal and commercially sensitive information.

His background in network operations also contributes to engagements involving system availability, incident escalation, user-access arrangements and business continuity. These considerations are important for organisations that rely on connected systems to deliver essential services and maintain day-to-day operations.

Femi’s work reinforces Amstel Consulting’s multidisciplinary approach by connecting privacy, compliance and governance requirements with the technology infrastructure needed to implement them effectively.

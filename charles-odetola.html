Charles Odetola

With over a decade of legal practice experience, Charles Odetola specialises in data protection, privacy, regulatory compliance and corporate governance, supporting organisations in building strong and sustainable privacy and compliance frameworks.

Professional Background & Practice Focus

Charles has advised organisations across the EU and international markets on navigating complex legal and regulatory requirements, developing privacy programmes, and shaping organisational policies and procedures in accordance with the GDPR, ePrivacy requirements and wider data protection standards.

His experience includes assessing organisations’ existing privacy and compliance environments against established risk frameworks, identifying regulatory and operational gaps, and supporting the implementation of appropriate technical and organisational measures (TOMs) to manage data protection and information security risks.

His broader expertise encompasses Data Privacy, Contract Management, Regulatory Compliance, Corporate Governance and Legal Risk Management. He combines legal and regulatory knowledge with a practical, risk-based approach, helping organisations translate complex compliance obligations into effective day-to-day business practices.

Charles is committed to helping organisations protect personal data, strengthen accountability and embed a genuine culture of privacy throughout their operations.

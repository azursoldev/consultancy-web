Under Section 33 and Section 39 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, data controllers and processors are obligated to ensure lawful, transparent, and fair processing of electronic telemetry and identifiers. Amstel Consulting operates as a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organization (DPCO) committed to the highest standards of digital trust.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and tracking pixels are, how we utilize them on our digital portals, and the precise controls available to you to accept, customize, or revoke your consent at any time without degrading essential advisory access.

  • Zero Third-Party Ad Networks: We do not monetize, cross-profile, or sell visitor browsing behaviors.
  • Granular Categorization: Strictly necessary, performance telemetry, and functional session preferences.
  • Statutory Consent Expiry: All consent records expire automatically after 12 months in compliance with NDPC guidelines.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small cryptographic text files placed on your browser or hardware device when you visit websites. They enable our web servers to recognize your session state, remember your navigation preferences, maintain secure form submissions for regulatory audit filings, and ensure responsive rendering across mobile and desktop environments.

In addition to HTTP cookies, we may utilize browser session storage (`sessionStorage` and `localStorage`) strictly to maintain your active search sessions, responsive mega-menu drawer states, and multi-step RFP submission drafts.

2. Categories of Cookies Deployed

A. Strictly Necessary Cookies: These tokens are essential for the website to function. They support site navigation, encrypted security protocols, session protection, and form validation for RFP inquiries. They cannot be disabled in our systems.

B. Performance & Diagnostic Telemetry: Aggregated, anonymized statistics that help us understand page latency, resource caching efficiency, and reader interaction with our statutory publications and CAR guidance whitepapers.

C. Functional & Preference Cookies: Allow the platform to remember your language settings, region-specific DPCO compliance checklists, and recent search filters within our statutory knowledge base.

Managing Your Browser Preferences

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can configure your browser settings to block or delete cookies across all visited websites. You can clear your cache, disable third-party cookies, or activate Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals at any time.

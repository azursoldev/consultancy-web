2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files or similar technologies that are stored on, or accessed through, your computer, smartphone, tablet or other device when you visit a website. Cookies allow websites to recognise a user’s device and may be used to:

  • enable website functionality;
  • maintain security;
  • remember preferences;
  • understand how visitors use a website;
  • improve website performance;
  • measure website traffic;
  • support communications or marketing; and
  • provide a more relevant user experience.

Some cookies are placed directly by us (“first-party cookies”), while others may be placed by third-party service providers whose technology is integrated into our Website (“third-party cookies”).

For purposes of this Notice, references to “cookies” include similar technologies that perform substantially similar functions.

3. Our Approach to Cookies

  • accept cookies;

  • select particular categories of cookies; and
  • subsequently change or withdraw your preferences.

Cookies that are strictly necessary for the basic operation, security or functionality of the Website may operate without optional cookie consent where permitted by applicable law.

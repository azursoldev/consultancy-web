Privacy Statement

ARTICLE 27 NDPA 2023 • STATUTORY NOTICE

Protecting Your Personal Data with Institutional Integrity.

Contact DPO Desk

At Amstel Consulting, safeguarding the confidentiality, integrity, and lawful processing of personal data is our primary institutional mission. As a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organization (DPCO) accredited by the National Data Protection Commission (NDPC), we adhere strictly to the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

This Privacy Statement outlines how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, engage our advisory services, attend executive masterclasses, or submit inquiries via our RFP desk.

  • Lawful Basis Compliance: All processing strictly anchored under Section 25 of the NDPA 2023.
  • No Secondary Commercialization: We never sell, rent, or lease personal identifiers to third-party data brokers.
  • Data Subject Rights Support: Streamlined protocols for access, rectification, objection, and erasure requests.

1. Categories of Data Collected

We collect personal identifiers necessary to evaluate compliance inquiries, prepare proposals, and maintain advisory communication. This includes your name, official job title, corporate email address, telephone number, organization name, and industry sector.

For statutory CAR audit engagements, we process departmental processing inventories (ROPA) and technical gap evidence strictly under bilateral non-disclosure agreements and encrypted storage architectures.

2. Your Rights Under NDPA 2023

Under Part VI of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, data subjects possess fundamental rights regarding their personal data, including the right to request access to held records, rectify inaccurate data, withdraw previously granted consent, and object to automated processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at `dpo@amstelconsulting.ng` or complete our dedicated advisory contact form. Our DPCO desk responds to all verified requests within the statutory timeline.

Technical Safeguards & Security Posture

All client data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols and safeguarded at rest with AES-256 standard encryption. Access is restricted through multi-factor authentication and role-based access controls.

Direct DPO Liaison & Regulatory Recourse

If you have unaddressed concerns regarding our processing activities, you also retain the statutory right under Section 43 to file a complaint directly with the National Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

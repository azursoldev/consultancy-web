Subhash Desai

Senior Advisory Board Member — Enterprise Risk & Governance

Subhash Desai is an experienced risk-management executive with more than 25 years of experience leading and managing risk. Based in London, he brings a senior international perspective to Amstel Consulting’s approach to enterprise risk, governance and organisational resilience.

Throughout his career, Subhash has developed extensive experience in identifying, assessing and managing risks within complex organisational environments. His perspective helps organisations understand how regulatory, operational, technological and information risks can affect business performance, continuity and stakeholder confidence.

As a Senior Advisory Board Member, Subhash advises Amstel Consulting on how data protection, cybersecurity and regulatory compliance should be considered within an organisation’s wider risk-management framework. His contribution helps the firm look beyond individual compliance obligations and consider how privacy and information risks interact with governance, internal controls, business strategy and executive decision-making.

For clients, this supports a more integrated and commercially practical approach to compliance. Rather than treating data protection as a separate legal or administrative exercise, Amstel Consulting helps organisations connect privacy responsibilities with risk ownership, organisational accountability, business continuity and senior-management oversight.

Subhash’s experience also strengthens the firm’s ability to communicate privacy, cybersecurity and technology risks in a way that is relevant to boards and senior executives. This helps clients distinguish significant risks from lower-priority concerns, allocate responsibility and make informed decisions about proportionate controls and investment.

Through his Advisory Board role, Subhash supports the continued development of Amstel Consulting’s risk-based methodologies, particularly for organisations operating in regulated, technology-enabled or rapidly changing environments. His insight helps ensure that recommendations reflect an organisation’s risk profile, operational realities and strategic priorities.

His contribution is particularly valuable where clients need to align data-protection compliance with enterprise risk management, internal audit, governance or organisational-resilience programmes. This enables Amstel Consulting to provide advice that supports regulatory accountability while also contributing to stronger and more sustainable business operations.

Subhash’s membership of the Advisory Board reinforces Amstel Consulting’s commitment to helping clients approach privacy and cybersecurity as business-governance issues, rather than isolated technical or legal requirements.

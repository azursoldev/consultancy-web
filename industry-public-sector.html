Public Sector & MDAs

Protecting sovereign national identity repositories, inter-agency data sharing conduits, and government cloud infrastructures across Federal Ministries, Departments, and Agencies.

Sovereign citizen data protection and national security

Government institutions and State Revenue Services are designated Data Controllers of Major Importance (DCMI) under the NDPA 2023. Sovereign voter registries, national identity databases, tax dossiers, and land registries demand the highest classification of defensive custody.

Amstel Consulting provides sovereign advisory to Federal Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs), designing classified data architectures, conducting statutory DPIAs on public-facing portals, and training senior civil servants on data stewardship.

Government Cloud Residency & Inter-Agency Data Sharing MoUs

Inter-agency collaboration between immigration, internal revenue, policing, and social welfare agencies often encounters constitutional friction regarding data sharing without explicit statutory authorization.

Our public sector practice drafts Inter-Agency Data Sharing Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs), validates sovereign cloud hosting residency, and structures compliant public open-data initiatives.

Citizen trust and digital e-government service portals

As state and federal services transition online—from passport renewals to motor vehicle licensing—citizen trust hinges on guaranteed privacy.

We audit e-government identity verification conduits, preventing data harvesting and verifying compliance before public portal launches.

Developing rigorous data classification tiers (Confidential, Secret, Top Secret) across federal and state ministerial databases.

Statutory MDA Annual CAR Audit Certification

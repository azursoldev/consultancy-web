These Terms and Conditions govern the provision of professional data protection compliance, statutory CAR audit assurance, cybersecurity risk audits, and outsourced Data Protection Officer (DPOaaS) services provided by Amstel Consulting to corporate and public sector clients.

By commissioning an advisory assignment, executing a Statement of Work (SOW), or submitting statutory audit mandates to our DPCO desk, the client acknowledges full acceptance of these terms, which operate in conjunction with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

  • Rigorous Confidentiality: Absolute NDA protection across client proprietary data structures and processing records.
  • Independent Audit Assurance: Objective evaluation pursuant to the NDPC General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID).
  • Statutory Filing Integrity: Assurance that all submissions meet official NDPC formatting, evidence thresholds, and deadlines.

1. Scope of DPCO Audit & CAR Services

Amstel Consulting shall perform the services outlined in the specific Statement of Work with professional skill, diligence, and integrity consistent with licensed Data Protection Compliance Organization standards.

Our statutory filings on behalf of data controllers of major importance include 5-domain GAID maturity scoring, gap remediation recommendations, and timely transmission to the National Data Protection Commission prior to the March 15 annual statutory deadline, provided the client furnishes required records in a timely manner.

2. Client Responsibilities & Disclosure

To ensure bulletproof regulatory assurance, the client agrees to provide Amstel Consulting auditors with complete, timely, and truthful access to processing registries (ROPA), technical system architectures, privacy notices, and designated internal privacy liaisons.

The client remains the designated Data Controller under the NDPA 2023. Amstel Consulting shall not be held liable for statutory penalties resulting from intentional omission, falsification, or withholding of material processing activities by the client.

Fees, Disbursements & Invoicing

Professional fees are structured on fixed-scope deliverables, tiered milestone payments, or annual outsourced retainers. Statutory filing levies payable directly to regulatory bodies (such as the NDPC CAR filing fees) are billed as direct client disbursements with full transparency.

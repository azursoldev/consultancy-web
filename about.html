Our Organisation: About Amstel Consulting

1. Leading with Integrity and Resilience

Amstel Consulting is a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO) and cybersecurity advisory firm helping organisations navigate data protection, privacy and digital security with confidence.

We combine regulatory expertise with practical business insight to translate complex data protection requirements into clear, workable solutions that support day-to-day operations. Our approach is grounded in integrity, accountability and operational resilience.

We believe effective data protection should enable business, not slow it down. By embedding privacy and security into business operations, we help organisations strengthen regulatory compliance, protect the rights of individuals, build stakeholder trust and create a more resilient foundation for sustainable growth.

2. Plain-Language Compliance and Corporate Strategy

Our approach is to make data protection, privacy and cybersecurity requirements clear, practical and actionable. We translate complex regulatory and technical obligations into straightforward strategies that organisations can understand, implement and maintain.

By bringing together data protection expertise, strategic advisory and technical security capabilities, we help organisations identify risk, strengthen internal controls, respond effectively to regulatory requirements and build more resilient data governance practices.

We work with discretion, transparency and professionalism, giving our clients the clarity and practical support they need to make informed decisions and manage an evolving digital and regulatory environment with confidence.

AMSTEL CONSULTING IS AN NDPC LICENSED DPCO

3. Our Credentials as a Licensed DPCO

As a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO), Amstel Consulting is authorised by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) to provide professional data protection compliance services, including consulting, compliance audits, training and regulatory support.

Our DPCO status enables us to conduct and verify Compliance Audit Returns (CAR) and facilitate their filing with the NDPC in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements. We work with organisations to assess their data protection practices, identify compliance gaps, strengthen internal governance and prepare the documentation required to demonstrate accountability.

This regulatory role is reinforced by multidisciplinary expertise across privacy, compliance and cybersecurity, allowing us to support clients beyond the filing itself and build practical data protection frameworks that work across their organisation.

4. A Multidisciplinary Team of Senior Advisory Experts

Our strength lies in the depth and diversity of our advisory expertise. Amstel Consulting brings together senior privacy professionals, cybersecurity specialists and corporate governance experts with experience supporting organisations across complex and highly regulated environments.

By combining regulatory, technical and strategic expertise within one advisory framework, we provide clients with a coordinated approach to data protection and cybersecurity. From board-level compliance and governance decisions to operational risk management and incident response, our specialists work together to address issues from every relevant perspective.

This multidisciplinary model gives organisations access to the right expertise when they need it—without managing multiple disconnected advisers—and enables us to deliver practical, commercially informed guidance across the full data protection and security lifecycle.

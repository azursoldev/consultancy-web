Data Processing Agreements

Statutory Section 29 & Vendor Supply Chain Governance

Shield Your Organization from Third-Party Vendor Vulnerabilities and Regulatory Liability.

Draft Enforceable DPAs

Under Section 29 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, data controllers cannot delegate their regulatory liabilities to external service providers without a legally binding, executed Data Processing Agreement (DPA). If your cloud host, payroll vendor, or SaaS platform suffers a breach, your organization remains accountable before the NDPC.

Amstel Consulting’s licensed DPCO legal practice drafts, reviews, and negotiates bespoke B2B Data Processing Agreements. We ensure strict technical security covenants, mandatory 72-hour breach reporting clauses, sub-processor authorization hierarchies, and cross-border adequacy protections.

  • Statutory Controller-to-Processor & Processor-to-Subprocessor Covenants
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for Cross-Border Data Flows (AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud)
  • Direct Regulatory Audit Rights, Post-Termination Data Erasure & Strict Indemnity Schedules

Banking & Capital Markets

Safeguarding institutional trust, core banking data integrity, and statutory compliance in an era of algorithmic finance and open banking directives.

Resilient, digital, and compliant financial architectures

As Nigerian commercial banks and merchant institutions accelerate cloud-native transformation and API integrations with fintechs, the regulatory surface expands exponentially. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, consumer financial information—including Bank Verification Numbers (BVN), transaction telemetry, and credit scoring inputs—demands end-to-end cryptographic safeguards.

Amstel Consulting acts as licensed DPCO partners to leading Tier-1 and Tier-2 lenders, structuring rigorous data mapping across legacy core banking engines (Finacle, Flexcube) and ensuring zero-trust data access architectures that withstand regulatory scrutiny and emerging threat vectors.

Mandatory Compliance Audit Return (CAR) & CBN Consumer Protection

The March 15 annual statutory CAR deadline mandates that all Data Controllers of Major Importance (DCMI) undergo exhaustive third-party verification across NDPC’s GAID 5-Domain framework. Concurrently, the Central Bank of Nigeria Consumer Protection Framework requires unequivocal proof of customer confidentiality and algorithmic dispute resolution mechanisms.

Our banking practice provides integrated compliance assurance: from identifying cross-border SWIFT data lineage to structuring defensive DPIAs for automated lending engines.

Value in security — How institutions scale safely

Data privacy is no longer merely a legal checkmark; it is the currency of institutional reputation in global capital markets. International correspondent banks and multilateral development partners scrutinize privacy governance prior to syndicating credit facilities or establishing credit lines.

We build end-to-end data governance operating models that empower credit and risk committees to innovate boldly while maintaining audit-ready documentation at every level of the balance sheet.

Core Banking Database Masking & BVN Safeguards

Deploy dynamic data masking (DDM) and tokenization across staging and non-production testing environments, preventing customer PII leaks during software releases.

Section 41 Cross-Border Cloud Banking Safeguards

Legally vetted adequacy assessments and Binding Corporate Rules (BCR) for banks hosting analytics or customer archives in foreign cloud nodes (AWS, Azure, Oracle Cloud).

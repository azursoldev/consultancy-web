Statutory protections for child and minor student data

Under NDPA Section 30, the personal data of a child or person lacking legal capacity enjoys heightened statutory immunity. Consent must be provided by parents or legal guardians, and any automated profiling or behavioural tracking of students is strictly restricted.

Amstel Consulting works with leading universities, private school consortia, and digital education platforms to implement robust parental consent mechanisms, student information system (SIS) security, and digital examination telemetry controls.

EdTech telemetry, AI proctoring & Student Records Retention

Modern EdTech platforms process extensive biometric telemetry during remote proctored exams: web camera video streams, keystroke patterns, and ambient audio. Without comprehensive DPIAs and strict retention limits, these operations invite massive regulatory liability.

Our education practice conducts DPIAs on educational software, structures student data destruction schedules, and prepares institutions for annual NDPC CAR statutory filings.

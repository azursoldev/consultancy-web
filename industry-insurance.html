Insurance & Underwriting

Navigating NAICOM cybersecurity directives, telematics driver monitoring, multi-decade statutory policy archives, and third-party loss investigator disclosures.

Underwriting precision and policyholder confidentiality

Insurance institutions process sensitive personal data across the entire lifecycle: medical records in life and health underwriting, vehicle GPS telematics in motor policies, and financial documentation in property claims. Balancing rigorous fraud detection with statutory data protection is paramount.

Amstel Consulting supports life and non-life underwriters, reinsurers, and insurance brokers in harmonizing NAICOM Market Conduct Directives with the statutory mandates of the NDPA 2023.

Loss Adjusters, Fraud Investigations & Legitimate Interests Assessments (LIA)

When claims arise, underwriters frequently engage third-party loss adjusters, forensic investigators, and private medical evaluators. Under the NDPA, transferring policyholder dossiers to these entities requires strict Data Processing Agreements.

Our team structures Legitimate Interests Assessments for anti-fraud surveillance, implements secure claims management workflows, and performs annual CAR audit certifications.

Multi-Decade Policy Retention & Safe Data Destruction

Life insurance and annuity contracts span decades, requiring data controllers to preserve policyholder, beneficiary, and trustee records over extended time horizons while safeguarding against data corruption.

We design secure cryptographic archiving protocols and automated data destruction workflows for lapsed and terminated policies.

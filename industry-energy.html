Energy, Oil & Power

Protecting Critical National Infrastructure (CNI), industrial SCADA operational telemetry, upstream workforce biometric records, and NUPRC/NERC compliance.

Critical national infrastructure meets cyber and data resilience

The energy sector represents the economic lifeblood of the nation. Upstream exploration platforms, LNG export terminals, pipeline SCADA telemetry, and electricity distribution companies (DisCos) process highly sensitive operational and personnel data.

Amstel Consulting delivers comprehensive data governance and cybersecurity assurance to multinational oil corporations, power generating companies (GenCos), and renewable energy developers, ensuring compliance with NUPRC, NERC, and NDPA mandates.

Offshore Worker Biometric Tracking & Subcontractor DPA Chains

Managing offshore logistics requires continuous biometric tracking of thousands of contractor personnel across offshore rigs and supply vessels. Without watertight Data Processing Agreements, prime operators face substantial breach exposure.

Our energy practice structures comprehensive vendor DPA governance, audits smart metering customer databases for electricity distribution utilities, and conducts annual CAR filings.

Explore Energy Sector Advisory

Smart Metering Telemetry & Customer Consumption Privacy

Smart grid electricity meters transmit high-frequency energy consumption data that can inadvertently reveal household occupancy, living habits, and security vulnerability.

We architect privacy-preserving customer metering data aggregation protocols for power distribution companies, preventing customer profiling risks.

Critical Infrastructure SCADA & Personnel Security

Auditing access permissions, remote vendor maintenance tunnels, and operator workstation logs across energy facilities.

Upstream Joint Venture CAR Audit Assurance

Preparing international oil companies and local energy operators for mandatory NDPC statutory compliance audit return certification.

Pension Fund Administrators (PFAs)

Securing lifetime Retirement Savings Accounts (RSA), biometric recapture databases, employer payroll conduits, and PENCOM regulatory audit filings.

Custodians of lifetime financial and biometric identities

Pension Fund Administrators (PFAs) and Closed Pension Fund Administrators (CPFAs) handle the long-term retirement destinies of millions of Nigerian contributors. The national Biometric Data Recapture Exercise has concentrated fingerprints, facial scans, National Identification Numbers (NIN), and payroll histories within PFA databases.

Amstel Consulting partners with leading PFAs and PFCs to ensure that RSA repositories meet the highest cryptographic standards, preventing identity theft and unauthorized pension fund liquidation.

PENCOM Regulatory Harmonization & Employer Payroll Lineage

PFAs receive regular data feeds from thousands of public and private employers. Ensuring the integrity of payroll conduits, avoiding unauthorized third-party disclosure, and managing beneficiary designations are high-stakes regulatory obligations.

We audit contribution pipelines, structure Data Transfer Agreements between employers and PFAs, and deliver licensed DPCO CAR filings before the statutory deadline.

Retirement payout verification & anti-impersonation controls

During pension withdrawal and programmed annuity payouts, biometric verification prevents phantom pensioner fraud while safeguarding the dignity of senior citizens.

Our team designs privacy-preserving biometric matching workflows and conducts exhaustive DPIAs on emerging self-service mobile pension portals.

RSA Biometric Recapture Security Audits

Penetration testing and database encryption reviews on biometric repositories containing fingerprint and facial template records.

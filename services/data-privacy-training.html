Data Privacy Training & Capacity Building

Human error remains a significant source of data protection and cybersecurity risk. The NDPA and GAID require organisations to implement appropriate organisational measures for data protection, including structured privacy sensitisation and training for relevant personnel.

Amstel Consulting provides customised, role-based data protection training programmes tailored to executive leadership, legal and compliance teams, HR professionals, customer-facing staff and IT teams.

Generic compliance presentations are rarely enough to build effective privacy practices. Our training programmes are designed by experienced data protection and compliance practitioners and use practical scenarios relevant to sectors including banking, fintech, healthcare and telecommunications. We translate complex regulatory requirements into clear, actionable practices that employees can apply in their day-to-day roles.

Role-Based Modular Curriculum

Effective privacy governance requires training that reflects the responsibilities and risks associated with different roles. Our customised modules include:

  • Board & C-Suite Masterclasses: Governance accountability, DCMI obligations and regulatory risk under the NDPA.
  • HR & People Operations: Lawful bases for employee data processing, payroll data protection and workplace privacy notices.
  • Marketing & Customer Support: Direct marketing requirements, consent and opt-out management, and Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs).
  • IT & Technical Teams: Privacy by design, access controls, data security and breach response and notification requirements.

Training Certification & Compliance Evidence

Upon completion, participants receive a certificate of completion, while your organisation receives a Training Compliance Dossier documenting the training delivered and participation.

This documentation can help demonstrate organisational accountability and provide supporting evidence of privacy training and capacity-building measures during applicable compliance assessments and Compliance Audit Return (CAR) processes. The current CAR framework expressly examines whether organisations have established capacity-building schedules and carried out the required training.

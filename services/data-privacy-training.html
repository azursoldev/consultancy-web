Data Privacy Training & Capacity Building

Human error is the single greatest vulnerability in corporate cybersecurity and data protection. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, organizations are legally required to ensure their personnel are adequately trained on data privacy principles, statutory rights of data subjects, and breach response duties. Amstel Consulting provides customized, accredited data protection training programs tailored to executive leadership, legal teams, HR officers, customer service staff, and IT administrators.

Generic compliance slide decks do not protect your business from regulatory fines or data breaches. Our training programs are designed by accredited DPCO lead practitioners with practical, real-world case studies from Nigeria's banking, fintech, healthcare, and telecommunications sectors. We transform complex legal articles into clear, actionable data handling habits that safeguard your brand.

Role-Based Modular Curriculum

Effective privacy governance requires targeted training for different operational units. Our customized modules include:

  • Board & C-Suite Masterclasses: Fiduciary risk, DCMI statutory mandates, and regulatory penalty mitigation under Section 48.
  • HR & People Operations: Lawful basis for employee data processing, payroll protection, and workplace privacy notices.
  • Marketing & Customer Support: Direct marketing consent requirements, opt-out management, and Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs).
  • IT & Technical Teams: Privacy-by-design architecture, role-based access control, and 72-hour incident response protocols.

Accredited Certification & Statutory Audit Evidence

Upon completion of our training programs, participants receive formal certification, and your organization receives an official Training Compliance Dossier. This documentation serves as critical, verifiable evidence of organizational accountability during your annual Compliance Audit Return (CAR) submission to the NDPC.

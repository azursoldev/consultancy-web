Recruiting, training, and retaining an in-house data privacy expert is both difficult and highly expensive. Amstel Consulting provides a seamless alternative: a fully outsourced DPO solution backed by an entire team of certified privacy professionals, legal experts, and cybersecurity engineers for a fraction of the cost of a full-time hire.

Pillar • Statutory Filing

Statutory Compliance Audit Return (CAR) Filing

The Business Goal:

Meet your mandatory annual legal obligations and avoid severe regulatory penalties.

What We Do: Under the NDPA, if your company handles data for over 2,000 individuals, you must file a return by March 15th annually. As a licensed DPCO, we manage your entire CAR roadmap—handling the complete verification, documentation prep, and official portal submission to secure your corporate compliance certificate smoothly.

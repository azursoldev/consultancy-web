File Your Annual Compliance Audit Return (CAR) with Confidence

Data Controllers and Data Processors that fall within the applicable categories under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025 may be required to file an annual Compliance Audit Return with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Where applicable, the filing is made through a licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO), which conducts the required compliance assessment and provides the necessary verification statement.

Amstel Consulting supports organisations throughout the process—from compliance assessment and remediation to preparation and submission of the applicable return.

Organisations that successfully complete the applicable compliance and filing requirements may be eligible for inclusion on the National Data Protection Adequacy Programme (NDPAP) Whitelist maintained by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Missing the annual March 31st filing deadline poses severe operational and financial risks to your organization. Failing to file your CAR on time automatically flags your business for direct, intrusive regulatory enforcement audits and exposes your company to heavy statutory fines calculated against your annual corporate turnover. Furthermore, a lack of a validated filing history can lead to regulatory blacklisting, meaning your organization could be disqualified from bidding on government contracts or passing mandatory vendor risk assessments.

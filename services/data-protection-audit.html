Comprehensive Data Protection Audit

A formal Data Protection Audit is a deep-dive evaluation of how your organization collects, stores, shares, and deletes personal information. For modern businesses, data is a highly valuable asset, but it is also a massive legal liability if managed incorrectly. Our comprehensive audit breaks down your entire organizational data flow, matching your current operational practices against the strict requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) to ensure you are fully insulated from legal and regulatory exposures.

Instead of overwhelming your staff with dense legal jargon, our licensed consultants deliver a clear, actionable roadmap. We assess your internal Record of Processing Activities (ROPA), analyze your Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs), and review your corporate privacy frameworks. We identify exactly where your data handling practices fall short, providing your management team with a prioritized, step-by-step remediation guide to fix vulnerabilities before they catch the attention of regulatory authorities.

Converting Compliance into Competitive Advantage

An un-audited business is highly vulnerable to catastrophic data leaks, employee errors, and severe financial penalties from the NDPC. By proactively auditing your systems with Amstel Consulting, you convert data compliance from an administrative chore into a major competitive advantage. Passing an independent, professional audit demonstrates to your investors, board of directors, and corporate clients that your organization operates at the highest global standards of data governance.

Non-Disruptive Collaborative Methodology

Our audit methodology is designed to be completely non-disruptive to your daily corporate activities. We work side-by-side with your legal, HR, and IT departments to collect information seamlessly, ensuring your business stays up and running throughout the engagement.

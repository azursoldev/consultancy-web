Work With Us to Strengthen Data Protection and Digital Trust

Amstel Consulting works with professionals and organisations that share our commitment to responsible data use, practical compliance and stronger digital governance.

We welcome interest from experienced professionals, emerging specialists, training partners, technology providers and organisations seeking strategic collaboration across Nigeria and other African markets.

Careers at Amstel Consulting

Our work requires professionals who can combine technical or regulatory knowledge with sound judgement, clear communication and an understanding of how organisations operate in practice.

Depending on our current needs, opportunities may be available in areas such as:

  • Data protection and privacy
  • Legal and regulatory compliance
  • Cybersecurity and information security
  • Corporate governance and risk
  • Compliance auditing
  • Data mapping and governance
  • Privacy programme implementation
  • Training and awareness
  • Research and regulatory analysis
  • Business development and client engagement
  • Project and administrative support

Opportunities may be offered on an employment, consultancy, internship, project or associate basis, depending on the role and applicable requirements.

What We Look For

We value professionals who demonstrate:

  • Integrity and respect for confidentiality
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Clear written and verbal communication
  • Attention to detail
  • Practical understanding of business operations
  • Commitment to professional development
  • Ability to work across disciplines
  • Respect for the rights and interests of data subjects
  • Willingness to challenge assumptions constructively
  • An understanding of professional independence and conflicts of interest

Relevant certifications and professional qualifications are welcomed, but qualifications alone are not sufficient. We also consider practical experience, judgement, communication skills and the ability to turn complex requirements into workable solutions.

We welcome expressions of interest from professionals with experience in:

  • Financial services

Inclusion in our professional network does not guarantee an assignment. All appointments are subject to due diligence, conflict checks, availability, competence and agreement on the applicable scope and terms.

Strategic Partnerships

We collaborate with organisations whose services can complement our privacy, governance and compliance work.

Potential partnerships may include:

Technology and Implementation Partners

Collaboration with responsible technology providers that support data discovery, consent management, compliance workflows, cybersecurity, records management or privacy operations.

Training and Academic Partners

Collaboration with universities, professional bodies, training providers and subject-matter experts to develop practical privacy, governance and cybersecurity education.

Professional and Advisory Firms

Collaboration with legal, audit, accounting, risk, governance, cybersecurity and consulting firms where a multidisciplinary approach would benefit the client.

Regional and International Partners

Collaboration with organisations and professionals that can support clients operating across African and international markets.

Referral Partners

Structured referral relationships with professional-service providers where client needs fall outside one party’s principal area of expertise.

Our Partnership Principles

We assess potential partnerships according to:

  • Professional competence
  • Integrity and reputation
  • Data-protection and security practices
  • Independence and conflict management
  • Quality of service
  • Transparency
  • Client benefit
  • Regulatory compatibility
  • Responsible use of technology
  • Alignment with our professional standards

A partnership will not be represented publicly until its nature, scope and respective responsibilities have been formally agreed.

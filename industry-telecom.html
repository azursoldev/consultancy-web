Telecommunications

Securing national subscriber identity registers (NIN-SIM), Call Detail Records (CDR), cell tower location telemetry, and mobile network operator compliance.

Mass subscriber connectivity and constitutional privacy rights

Telecommunications operators maintain the largest real-time databases in the nation: National Identification Number (NIN) linked subscriber identities, cell tower triangulation telemetry, roaming logs, and Call Detail Records (CDR). Under the NDPA 2023, these databases represent critical national data assets.

Amstel Consulting advises Tier-1 Mobile Network Operators (MNOs), tower infrastructure operators (TowerCos), and Internet Service Providers (ISPs) on navigating lawful interception protocols and NCC consumer codes.

NIN-SIM Data Harmonization & Third-Party Value-Added Services (VAS)

VAS aggregators and content providers frequently interface with core telecommunications carrier subscriber directories. Uncontrolled disclosure of subscriber phone numbers to marketing aggregators constitutes a severe statutory violation.

We audit VAS integration pipelines, implement zero-trust access control for external vendors, and prepare nationwide network carriers for annual statutory CAR certification.

5G Network Slicing & Real-Time Location Telemetry

As 5G rollouts expand across major commercial metropolitan centers, network slicing and micro-location tracking enable hyper-granular consumer surveillance.

Our team conducts specialized technical DPIAs on 5G network components, establishing differential privacy buffers that prevent unauthorized individual tracking.

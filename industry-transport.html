Transport & Logistics

Governing telematics surveillance, real-time GPS fleet tracking, cross-border passenger manifests, and automated courier dispatching across aviation, maritime, and freight.

Connected fleets, automated dispatch, and mobility privacy

Transport operations generate continuous geographic telemetry: driver locations, passenger travel history, delivery addresses, and border crossing manifests. Under the NDPA 2023, continuous GPS tracking constitutes personal surveillance and demands explicit legal justification.

Amstel Consulting advises commercial airlines, ride-hailing networks, maritime cargo handlers, and nationwide trucking operators on aligning fleet telematics with employee rights and statutory data protection.

International Passenger Name Records (PNR) & Cross-Border Adequacy

Airlines operating regional and international flights transmit Passenger Name Records (PNR) and Advance Passenger Information (API) across sovereign borders daily. Harmonizing ICAO regulations with NDPA Section 41 transfer rules requires specialized advisory.

Our transport practice structures cross-border airline data transfer frameworks, conducts DPIAs on facial recognition boarding gates, and certifies compliance with the NCAA.

Maritime logistics & customs data clearing security

Port operators and maritime shippers handle high-value cargo manifests containing consignee identity documents, customs declarations, and banking references.

We secure automated terminal operating systems (TOS) and implement role-based access controls that protect commercial confidentiality and personal data.

Fleet Telematics & Driver Privacy Notices

Drafting compliant employee monitoring disclosures and privacy notices for fleet tracking systems without compromising operational safety.
